Maned Wolf

Maned Wolf Habitat: Grasslands and scrub forests of South America (Brazil, Paraguay, Argentina).

Diet: Omnivorous — eats small mammals, birds, and fruit (especially “wolf apples”).

Special Feature: Long legs adapted for tall grass; fox-like appearance.

Behavior: Solitary, communicates with loud “roar-barks.”

Threats: Road accidents, habitat fragmentation, and hunting.
